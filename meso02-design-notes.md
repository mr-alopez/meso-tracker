# Meso 02 — Design Notes

22 Sep 2026 · block rationale, per cue policy §5. Not shown in the app.

---

## What Meso 01 showed

- **Friday was logged one week of four.** Everything that lived only on Friday got
  almost nothing — hamstrings saw five sets across the block.
- **Sessions overran the morning window.** The last exercises on Wednesday and Friday
  were cut repeatedly.
- **Anterior shoulder and collarbone discomfort escalated across the block,** spreading
  from one side to both and across upright rows, shoulder press, lateral raises,
  pushdowns and the standard pulldown. Pain flags on upright row (week 2) and shoulder
  press (week 3).
- **Systemic fatigue clustered on the squat day** — nausea, cardio ramp, gassed by the
  end.
- **The priority lift never moved.** Incline press held at 25 all block while reps
  reached 12 on every set by week 3 — the v1.9 gate holding it. It was also done third
  on Monday, behind the flye and the shoulder press.

---

## Structure

Push/pull/legs gave each day sole ownership of its muscles, so a missed day deleted
them. Meso 02 puts every muscle's core work on the two days that reliably happen.

| Day | Role | Sets (wk 1) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | A — full coverage, upper chest first | 16 |
| Wed | B — full coverage | 16 |
| Fri | C — optional, second exposures only | 10 |

Sixteen sets runs about 40 minutes at Meso 01's measured all-in pace (~2.4 min/set).
Meso 01's week 2 Monday was 18 sets in 43 minutes.

**Five weeks, RIR 3 / 3 / 2 / 1 / deload.** The extra week sits at the easy end, buying
exposures without adding a second week near failure.

**The lead lift adds its set in week 2**, while RIR holds at 3, then holds volume as
intensity tightens. Volume and intensity don't both rise in the same week.

| Hamstrings | 3 | 5 |
| Triceps | 2 | 2 |
| Side delt | 0 | 0 |

Lower than Meso 01's plan, and deliberately so. Meso 01's planned volume never
happened. Upper chest now gets two core exposures a week instead of one, and the v2.0
gate lets its load move.

Triceps are low on direct work; incline pressing supplies indirect volume.

## Shoulder exclusions — pending assessment

Removed until a physio has seen the shoulders:

- Upright rows (pain flag, week 2)
- Overhead pressing (pain flag, week 3)
- Lateral raises (collarbone-to-shoulder sensation, both sides, week 3)

Neutral-grip substitutions where a choice existed: hammer curl for incline curl,
rope for bar pushdown, neutral-grip pulldown kept from the mid-block swap.

Reintroducing any of these is a PROGRAM edit, not a swap — they aren't in the block, so
there is nothing to swap from.

**Also removed on your own notes:** barbell good mornings. Fatigue pushed the strain
into the lower back. Cable pull-through covers the hinge on Friday with much less
spinal loading.

## Starting loads

Most seed automatically from Meso 01's last loading week. Three explicit starts override
the seed:

| Exercise | Start | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral-grip pulldown | 70 | Meso 01's only week was a ramp (50/60/70/70), so no working load is computable. 70 is what the working sets used |
| Seated leg curl | 140 | Same — a ramp from 120. Your note: "found the weight at 140" |
| Machine flye | 70 | The seed would be 80, which your week 3 note and a final set of 6 both say was too much |

New to this block, starting from find-it: low-to-high cable flye, hammer curl, rope
pushdown, cable rear delt flye, cable pull-through, cable curl. Their cues expand
automatically until first logged.

Rows seed at 15 lb and stay there until the gate unlocks 20. That's conservative on
purpose: the chest-supported row was landing in the shoulders, which reads as a
technique problem that more load would make worse.

## Not included

**Calves.** The library entries were proposed but not yet added. Once they exist, one
calf exercise fits on Friday.

## What to watch

- Whether Monday and Wednesday actually finish inside the window. If the last
  exercise still gets cut, the answer is removing it, not rushing.
- Incline press loading to 30 lb. It's the one heavy pressing movement left, and the
  shoulders' response to that jump is the most important signal in the block.
- How often Friday happens. Whatever the answer, the block doesn't depend on it.
